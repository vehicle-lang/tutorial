# Courses taught using the Vehicle Tutorial

This tutorial has been run live and adapted for different audiences at:

- [FOMLAS'23 the 6th Workshop on Formal Methods for ML-Enabled Autonomous Systems](https://fomlas2023.wixsite.com/fomlas2023) co-allocated with CAV'23 in Paris, France; 18 July 2023;

- [VETSS Summer School](https://wp.doc.ic.ac.uk/vss/) at the University of Surrey, UK; 23 August 2023; [Youtube recording available](https://youtu.be/-ZRE1BgaCk0);

- [ICFP'23 the International Conference on Functional Programming](https://icfp23.sigplan.org/) in Seattle, USA; 08 September 2023.

- [2024-2026 Dependable and Deployable AI for Robotics, CDT](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/), Edinburgh and Heriot-Watt university PhD level course.

- [2025 SAT/SMT Summer School](https://sat-smt-ar-school.gitlab.io/www/2025/), St Andrews University, 6-8 August 2025.

We thank all our readers and attendees for their feedback and contribution.
This [FOMLAS'23 tutorial outline](https://github.com/vehicle-lang/tutorial/blob/source/FoMLAS2023.pdf) gives a summary of all tutorial resorces.
